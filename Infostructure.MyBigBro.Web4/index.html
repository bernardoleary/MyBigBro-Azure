MyBigBro — Motorway Journey Infographic

MyBigBro turns motorway cameras in Auckland (NZTA) and London (TfL JamCams) into an interactive route infographic:

  • 🎢 Journey Deck (Drag Up/Down): Drag up/down (or scroll/use arrow keys) to glide camera-by-camera along your motorway journey with live distance & travel-time telemetry.
  • 📊 Full Spine Infographic: View the entire motorway corridor as a continuous highway diagram with live video/time-lapse nodes.
  • 🇳🇿 Auckland (15-Min Loop) & 🇬🇧 London (Live MP4 Video): Switch cities or star (★) cameras to build your custom door-to-door commute.
