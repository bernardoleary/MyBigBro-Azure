About MyBigBro Commute Strip & Camera Replay

MyBigBro turns static traffic cameras into an instant Commute Corridor Strip for Auckland (NZTA) and London (TfL JamCams).

  • Auckland (310 NZTA Cameras): Automatically buffers a rolling 15-minute time-lapse loop on major motorway corridors (SH1, SH16, SH18, SH20) so you can immediately see whether traffic is flowing or stationary.
  • London (890 TfL JamCams): Plays simultaneous live video loops along the A406 North Circular, Blackwall Tunnel / A13, A4/M4 & A40 Westway, and Central Thames Bridges.
  • PWA Drive Mode: Keep your screen awake on a dash mount to automatically capture motorway snapshots as you drive past cameras.
